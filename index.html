10:00 AM – 12:00 PM
Live models, datasets and phantoms - practice volume imaging
  • Acquire a volume with a narrow B and C plane.
  • Acquire a volume of a BPD, then move only the axis dot into the vermis of the cerebellum and then into the CSP.
  • Select different render modes, like VSI and add a Chroma.
  • In a fetal face volume with RealisticVue applied, create a different hue.
  • In a volume with CrystalVue applied, select Circle ROI.
  • If scanning a volunteer, scan a placenta with MV-Flow and acquire a volume.
  • Make a few volume preset changes and save into the User 1 preset.
  • Acquire a XI STIC if the fetus is cooperative, then select a Front Back Render direction and render a 4Chamber view.
  • If fetus is not cooperative, pull up an STIC dataset, change to Front Back Render direction and render a 4Chamber View. Then open up the ROI box in the B plane and place the Render Line at the level of the 3VV.
  • Acquire a sagittal spine and change to a Left/Right or Right/Left render direction.
  • On a volume of the spine, apply Multi-Slice View, turn on the Central View, then shift through the slices and change the thickness.
  • On a volume of the spine, activate ObliqueVue, use polyline to display slices and turn on OVIX. Select Plumb line and draw through the spine. Then do the same with Parallel line. (Hint: You need to be on six on one or nine on one to make Plumb line and Parallel line active.)
